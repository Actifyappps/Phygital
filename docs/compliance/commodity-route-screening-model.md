# Commodity–destination–buyer route screening model

**Source:** Deliverable 008 and Compliance Validation 004. This is a screening structure, not legal clearance or an export approval.

## Decision unit
The decision unit is a specific **product form × destination × buyer requirement × route × exporter capacity × provider pathway**. A commodity name or destination country alone is insufficient.

## Candidate commodities carried for investigation
- Tea (including orthodox and purple tea possibilities).
- Honey (appropriately specified premium honey).
- Avocados.

These are candidate investigation areas only. No commodity, destination, buyer, exporter, hub, producer group or regulatory pathway is confirmed by the cited working records.

## Required profile
Product form, grade, processing, intended use, packaging, quantity/volume, origin, seasonality, shelf-life, buyer specification, sample/inspection requirements, destination, route, Incoterm/delivery basis, importer requirements, competent authorities, certificates/documents, lab/inspection needs, customs process, payment pathway, insurance, responsible legal entity, source citations, source date, reviewer and open questions.

## Rule lifecycle
Draft → source checked → specialist review → approved for defined scope and effective period → monitored → superseded/expired/withdrawn. Every rule has an authoritative source, version/date, commodity and product-form scope, destination/route scope, applicability conditions, owner/reviewer and evidence. Unknown, stale or contradictory returns HOLD / human review, not pass.

## Hard stop conditions
No competent source; no valid exporter capacity; unclear importer requirements; missing mandatory document/certificate; facility scope mismatch; payment/provider legality unresolved; route or insurance unavailable; product integrity issue; or unresolved high-severity evidence discrepancy.

Never automate legal advice, certification, customs release or a final export eligibility determination.
