# Commodity–destination–buyer route screening model

**Source:** Deliverable 008 and Compliance Validation 004. This is a screening structure, not legal clearance or an export approval.

## Decision unit
The decision unit is a specific **product form × destination × buyer requirement × route × exporter capacity × provider pathway**. A commodity name or destination country alone is insufficient.

## Candidate commodities carried for investigation
- Tea (including orthodox and purple tea possibilities).
- Honey (appropriately specified premium honey).
- Avocados.

These are candidate investigation areas only. No commodity, destination, buyer, exporter, hub, producer group or regulatory pathway is confirmed by the cited working records.

## Required profile
Product form, grade, processing, intended use, packaging, quantity/volume, origin, seasonality, shelf-life, buyer specification, sample/inspection requirements, destination, route, Incoterm/delivery basis, importer requirements, competent authorities, certificates/documents, lab/inspection needs, customs process, payment pathway, insurance, responsible legal entity, source citations, source date, reviewer and open questions.

## Rule lifecycle
Draft → source checked → specialist review → approved for defined scope and effective period → monitored → superseded/expired/withdrawn. Every rule has an authoritative source, version/date, commodity and product-form scope, destination/route scope, applicability conditions, owner/reviewer and evidence. Unknown, stale or contradictory returns HOLD / human review, not pass.

## Hard stop conditions
No competent source; no valid exporter capacity; unclear importer requirements; missing mandatory document/certificate; facility scope mismatch; payment/provider legality unresolved; route or insurance unavailable; product integrity issue; or unresolved high-severity evidence discrepancy.

Never automate legal advice, certification, customs release or a final export eligibility determination.


## Framework 008 screening gates and scoring

### Evidence classes
Use the canonical E1–E4 definitions in [the evidence and provenance model](../data/evidence-and-provenance-model.md). E1–E4 is a claim-evidence strength class, not acquisition type.

### Twelve hard-stop gates
A candidate cannot be recommended for a live pilot while any critical gate is failed or unresolved. It may remain in discovery but must be labelled HOLD.

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| H1 Buyer need | Real buyer or credible channel supplies a specific product requirement |
| H2 Product legality | Product and proposed route are not prohibited; applicable permissions identified |
| H3 Exporter responsibility | Legally suitable exporter of record identified and willing |
| H4 Supply credibility | Identified producers and realistic volumes evidenced |
| H5 Product conformity | Buyer/destination requirements can be met and tested |
| H6 Traceability and custody | Batch identity and custody can be maintained through handover |
| H7 Physical handling | Aggregation, storage, packing and handling can be lawfully/safely performed |
| H8 Logistics feasibility | Viable route, carrier/forwarder and handling plan evidenced |
| H9 Payment/funds flow | Lawful payer/payee/provider route and reconciliation defined |
| H10 Commercial viability | Plausible economics with transparent fees and risk allocation |
| H11 Data/privacy | Data use, access, retention and sharing appropriately controlled |
| H12 Dispute/failure handling | Rejection, delay, loss, quality dispute, non-payment and cancellation assigned |

“Unverified” is not a finding of non-compliance; it means evidence has not been obtained or assessed. Never promote it to Pass based on assumption or verbal assurance.

### Weighted case evaluation

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Buyer demand and purchase credibility | 15 |
| Supply reliability and aggregation potential | 12 |
| Regulatory and standards pathway clarity | 15 |
| Product quality and conformity readiness | 10 |
| Logistics, shelf life and handling | 12 |
| Unit economics and value distribution | 12 |
| Traceability and hub operational fit | 8 |
| Pilot complexity and time to readiness | 8 |
| Strategic learning and repeatability | 8 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Score bands: 80–100 strong candidate only if all hard stops pass; 65–79 promising but conditional; 50–64 weak/high dependency; below 50 not suitable on current evidence. A score never overrides a failed or unresolved hard-stop gate. Prefer stronger E1/E2 evidence, fewer critical dependencies, clearer buyer acceptance and lower operational complexity where candidates score similarly.
