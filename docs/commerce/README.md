# Commerce and commercial service model

This section owns the platform's service catalogue, commercial model, fee/billing logic, transaction charges, refunds, cost allocation and transparent producer-proceeds presentation. It is deliberately separate from payment-provider integration and from the seller's underlying sale contract.

Primary sources: Concept Paper v1.1 (Commercial Model), Deliverable 003, Deliverable 009, Sponsor Decision Pack 018-A, 024 and audit 024-R F-05/F-09/F-11.

No fee schedule, payer, commission, tax treatment, hub share or provider share is approved by this folder. Keep implementation disabled until sponsor, legal and tax review and supporting evidence are recorded.
