# Commercial model validation

**Source:** Deliverable 003, 009 and 018-A. Status: design requirements only; no final fee schedule, provider contract or viability finding.

## Model components to validate
- Who pays for each service and why.
- Who provides the service, bears its cost and carries liability.
- Which services are platform-coordinated versus delivered by a hub, provider, seller or exporter.
- Producer-facing burden and whether any service is funded/subsidised by sponsor or programme.
- Listing, verification, documentation, hub-service, inspection, logistics and other fees only where the service and payer are validated.
- Transaction-level cost-to-serve, gross margin, operating overhead, working capital and cash timing.
- Producer gross sale value, deductions, fees and net proceeds, shown transparently.
- Currency, exchange-rate source/time, tax treatment, refunds, cancellations, chargebacks and disputes.
- Separation between platform service fees and underlying buyer-to-seller sale proceeds.

## Guardrails
D-01 platform coordinator only; no default seller/payee. D-07 direct buyer-to-seller payment. Platform must not receive, hold, pool or transmit sale funds unless separately authorised. No default escrow, wallet or payout capability.

## Minimum evidence
Costed service catalogue; payer interviews/acceptance; partner quotations; documented unit economics; legal/tax review; provider confirmation; sensitivity analysis; producer/buyer value evidence; sponsor approval. A placeholder fee model is not a commitment or approved pricing.
