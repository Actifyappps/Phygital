# Pilot maturity and software delivery are separate axes

**Source:** 024-R F-03; 019, 020, 021 and 024-G.  
**Status:** Clarification of status vocabulary; not an advancement decision.

## Axis A — physical/pilot maturity
- Level 1: digital readiness rehearsal using synthetic data.
- Level 2: controlled physical batch demonstration, only with separately approved scope and safeguards.
- Level 3: partner-led micro-export, only after route, legal, commercial, operational and regulatory gates pass.

Use the exact current Level 1–3 definitions in 020 and the governing project records; do not infer permission from this summary.

## Axis B — software delivery maturity
Specification readiness; component/contract readiness; integrated workflow readiness; operational acceptance readiness; production/pilot authorization. 024-G defines the detailed G0–G4 gates.

## Independence rule
Passing a software gate does not advance physical pilot maturity. Completing a physical demonstration does not prove production software readiness. A decision record must state both axes, evidence references, scope, conditions and authorised approver.

## Exact Level 1–3 boundary from Deliverables 019–021

- **Level 1 — Digital readiness rehearsal:** synthetic or properly authorised sample data only. Tests usability, workflow, data capture and document reuse. It does not prove real supply, buyer willingness, physical quality or export readiness.
- **Level 2 — Controlled physical batch demonstration:** one real batch at an existing site, with producer authority and written site permission, service-only site scope unless separately authorised, relevant competent checks and buyer feedback. No platform custody, live sale, payment or export dispatch.
- **Level 3 — Partner-led micro-export:** a qualified seller/exporter and providers execute a specific real transaction under confirmed legal, regulatory, commercial and sponsor gates. Platform remains coordinator and does not hold funds.

The phrase in Deliverable 019 referring to a small real batch belongs to Level 2; it does not alter the synthetic-only Level 1 plan in 020–023.
