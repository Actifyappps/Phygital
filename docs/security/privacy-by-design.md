# Privacy-by-design implementation checklist

- Identify personal/commercial data elements and their purposes before collecting them.
- Minimise fields, attachments, exports, logs and analytics.
- Define controller/processor roles and lawful basis only after qualified review.
- Separate public product information from restricted organisation, member, quotation and transaction data.
- Apply tenant/resource-level access checks on every read and write path.
- Restrict bulk export, search enumeration and document download.
- Define access, correction, retention, deletion, legal-hold and breach workflows.
- Avoid real participant data in development, demos, fixtures and public issue trackers.
- Review cross-border hosting, international transfers, provider subprocessors and destination-market obligations.
- Provide understandable notices and user-facing explanations for verification and evidence status.
- Keep consent and communication preferences purpose-specific; consent is not a substitute for another required legal basis.
- Conduct a privacy impact assessment or equivalent review when the applicable threshold and legal advice require it.

This is a design checklist, not a legal compliance determination or approved privacy notice.
