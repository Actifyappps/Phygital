**Purpose:** Obtain a complete, provenance-verifiable source artifact or an authorised formal disposition. This brief does not appoint a custodian, authorise external circulation or change document authority.

## 1. Why this request exists

A draft titled **“Phygital Export Marketplace: Developer Guide”**, Version 0.1, dated 17 November 2026 and prepared for Actify Inc., is referenced in prior project material. The complete source file/page, provenance, approval state and version integrity have not been recovered from the accessible sources reviewed.

The repository must not infer the contents of that guide from a partial excerpt, a prototype, a remembered stack description or a secondary reference. The current hierarchy remains:

1. Concept Paper v1.1 — strategic baseline, with its stated draft-for-partner-discussion status.
2. Technical Development Manual v3.0 — supporting technical/operational planning.
3. Phase 0 validation and evidence artefacts — validate assumptions and constrain delivery.
4. Developer Guide v0.1 — authority unresolved until the source and its status are verified.
5. Technical artefacts and candidate architecture — subject to the approved decision gates.

## 2. Request to the source custodian

Please provide one of the following outcomes.

### Outcome A — complete source recovered

Supply the original complete document or native page export, preserving:

- complete title page and all body content;
- author/preparer and intended owner/custodian;
- exact version, date and document status;
- revision/version history, if maintained;
- approval, review, circulation or supersession record;
- appendices, diagrams, tables, code/configuration examples and referenced attachments;
- source location and retrieval date;
- any known copies or derivative versions, with their relationship to the original.

Do not re-create a missing document and label it as the original. If only a copy is available, identify it as a copy and state its provenance and known limitations.

### Outcome B — formal unavailability or supersession disposition

If the complete source cannot be located, provide an authorised written disposition that states:

- the identity and authority of the person making the disposition;
- searches or repositories checked and their date;
- whether the document is unavailable, withdrawn, superseded, advisory-only or never formally issued;
- whether any partial extracts or derivative copies are known;
- whether any decisions previously attributed to the guide remain valid, are withdrawn, or require fresh approval;
- which source documents govern instead;
- whether a replacement guide is required and who is authorised to commission it.

A statement that the document cannot currently be found is a search result, not by itself an approval to supersede it.

## 4. Required reconciliation after recovery

A recovered document is not automatically authoritative. Review it against:

- Concept Paper v1.1;
- Technical Development Manual v3.0;
- TSBBF v1.0;
- AADDG v1.0;
- 024-A–G and approved sponsor decisions;
- current ADR-0001 and ADR-0002;
- Phase 0 route, site, actor, privacy and commercial evidence.

For every material statement, classify it as one of:

1. Strategic/product requirement.
2. Domain or workflow requirement.
3. Technical design proposal.
4. Candidate technology or vendor suggestion.
5. Approved decision, with decision reference.
6. Implementation instruction.
7. Assumption or forecast.
8. External dependency or partner commitment.
9. Unsupported, ambiguous or superseded statement.

Produce a conflict/precedence schedule. Record exact source section/page, conflict, governing authority, proposed resolution, decision owner and decision status. Do not silently merge contradictory provisions.
