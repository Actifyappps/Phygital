# Marketplace trust, listing integrity, abuse reporting and appeals

**Sources:** 024-R F-06; 024-E; 024-F sections 9–10; 024-G; 024-B.  
**Status:** Required design capability; operational policy and production controls remain subject to review.

## 1. Public trust boundary
The public marketplace must present only an allow-listed projection. It must explain the coordinator role and the difference between declared, documentary and independently verified claims. It must not imply certification, guaranteed export eligibility, guaranteed buyers, escrow protection or institutional endorsement without evidence.

## 2. Listing integrity
- Require valid seller capacity and current backing-batch eligibility before publication.
- Detect duplicate, stale, withdrawn, incompatible or overcommitted supply.
- Version material listing changes and trigger re-review where required.
- Display last-updated time, evidence scope/limitations and availability source.
- Do not expose private documents, producer member identities, restricted batch details or internal case data through public pages or QR resolution.
- Search ranking is not an endorsement or buyer suitability decision.

## 3. Reporting and case workflow
Provide report routes for fake identities, forged authority, misrepresented origin/grade/quantity/evidence, duplicate supply, manipulation, harassment, coercion, bribery, collusion, payment/shipment spoofing, spam and privacy violations.

A case records unique ID, type, severity, subject/resource scope, reporter confidentiality, evidence, owner role, decision authority, status, timestamps, temporary restrictions, review date, outcome and linked appeal. Open reports are not findings of wrongdoing.

## 4. Fair process and participant protection
- Restrict case access to need-to-know roles.
- Use proportionate, time-bounded interim restrictions with documented authority and review.
- Require human review for material restrictions and decisions.
- Separate investigator/decision-maker from subject and reporter where practicable.
- Give reasoned outcomes and a correction/appeal route, subject to lawful confidentiality and safety constraints.
- Protect reporters and participants against retaliation.
- Record reinstatement, correction and lessons learned.

## 5. Required product surfaces
Public process/trust information; listing report action; buyer/supplier case inbox; operations triage; evidence review; restriction notice; appeal submission/status; reviewer decision and audit history.

## 6. Tests
Public/QR projection leakage; false certification claims; stale evidence; fake seller/exporter; duplicate listing; spam; malicious report; retaliation; investigator conflict; appeal by restricted participant; improper permanent suspension; listing reinstatement while source batch remains held.
