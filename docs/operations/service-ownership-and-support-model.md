# Product operations, service ownership and participant support

**Sources:** 003, 010, 011, 012, 019; 024-R F-11; 024-A, 024-E, 024-F and 024-G.  
**Status:** Operating-design requirement; no staff appointments, partner service levels or production support commitments are implied.

## 1. Service ownership
For each participant-facing and internal service, define accountable organisation, responsible role, service boundary, operating hours, channel, response target, escalation, cost owner, evidence and continuity arrangement. A job title or role placeholder is not an appointment.

## 2. Minimum service catalogue
Participant onboarding assistance; producer-assisted intake; hub service coordination; evidence review; buyer enquiry support; listing integrity; quotation/order administration; document coordination; exception and complaint handling; account/access support; privacy/security incident intake; offline sync support.

## 3. Responsibility and separation
Keep platform coordination separate from seller/exporter obligations, custody, laboratory certification, customs decisions, carrier obligations and payment processing. Define who performs, approves, reviews, pays and is liable for each service before live operation.

## 4. Support and escalation
Define intake channel, severity, acknowledgement/response/resolution targets, business-hours coverage, escalation owner, evidence retention and fallback. Do not publish SLAs until staffing, providers, costs and operating hours are confirmed.

## 5. Continuity and stop conditions
Document unavailable-site procedure, staff absence, connectivity failure, data discrepancy, authority expiry, security incident and provider outage. If a material fact or authority cannot be verified, stop the dependent action and preserve evidence.

## 6. Acceptance
Test handoff clarity, unresolved case escalation, role separation, support continuity, participant accessibility, response measurement and cost per service. Numeric service levels require measured baseline and sponsor approval.
