# Field and hub operating model — digital control boundary

## Purpose
Support the phygital loop while preserving the distinction between a service facility and a custodian, seller or exporter.

## Baseline hub services (subject to site agreement)
Potentially record appointments, identity checks, intake observations, weighing/measurement, sampling handoff, document capture, aggregation support and digital assistance. The actual service list must be agreed with each site operator and validated for capability, equipment, staffing and safety.

## Hub must not be assumed to
- Own, buy, sell or export product.
- Take custody or title to goods.
- Hold stock, reserve it, release it or dispatch it.
- Certify product quality or regulatory compliance without the relevant competence/accreditation.
- Act as a customs office, laboratory, financial institution or payment provider.

## Service event minimum record
Facility/service capability, site and time, operator identity and authority, organisation scope, batch reference, activity performed, method/equipment, unit and result where applicable, evidence/provenance type, limitations, exception and reviewer.

## Physical handoff
Any future custody/handover model requires separate sponsor decision, contract, legal review, insurance/liability allocation, facility controls, SOP, inventory movement model and acceptance tests. A digital allocation is not a handover record.
