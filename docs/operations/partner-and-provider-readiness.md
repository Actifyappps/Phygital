# Partner, facility and provider readiness

**Sources:** 010 Hub & Facility Readiness Assessment; 011 Operating Blueprint; 013 validation pack; 024; 024-D; 024-R F-11.  
**Status:** readiness model, not procurement approval, appointment, contract or partner commitment.

## Record dimensions
- Legal identity, ownership, signatory and authority.
- Exact service capability, geography, commodity/product form and exclusions.
- Licence/accreditation scope, issuing authority, validity and expiry where applicable.
- Staffing, competence, equipment, calibration/maintenance and operating capacity.
- Infrastructure, connectivity, power, safety, hygiene, accessibility and safeguarding.
- Insurance/liability, incident/emergency process and continuity.
- Indicative cost, pricing basis, service hours, capacity and service levels.
- Data-processing role, confidentiality, security, access, retention and incident notification.
- Contract, subcontract, audit rights, complaints, escalation and exit/transition.
- Evidence provenance, reviewer, effective dates, expiry and reassessment history.

## Lifecycle
Candidate → evidence requested → assessed → remediation required / unsuitable / conditionally ready → approved for a precisely named scope → contracted/appointed (separate decision) → active → suspended / expired / withdrawn.

A facility's existence or ICT hub status does not establish authority to handle goods. A provider's profile or licence does not establish that the licence covers the exact service, commodity, geography or date. Hard-stop failures cannot be averaged away by a score.

## Digital support
The system may store readiness assessments, source evidence, scope, conditions, review decisions, expiry alerts and corrective actions. It must not create a partner appointment, award procurement, grant legal authority or imply availability without separate approval and evidence.

## Integration readiness
Before an adapter is activated, confirm accountable owner, contract, lawful data-sharing basis, technical credentials, sandbox access, callback verification, idempotency, retry/reconciliation, support hours, incident escalation, service levels, data retention, security review and exit plan.
