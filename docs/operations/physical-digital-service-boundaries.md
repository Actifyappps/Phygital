# Physical–digital service boundaries

**Purpose:** prevent the marketplace's digital records from overstating physical or legal facts.

| Activity | Digital record can establish | Digital record alone cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Producer declaration | Who declared what, when, quantity, unit and source | Existence, ownership, quality or availability of goods |
| Hub appointment | Planned service, location, time and assigned operator | Facility authority or acceptance of custody |
| Intake record | Operator-recorded observation and evidence | Independent verification or title transfer |
| Device measurement | Instrument reading, device identity/calibration metadata and time | Product grade, certification or legal acceptance |
| Independent inspection | Inspector's recorded result within documented competence/scope | Broader certification or export clearance |
| Batch creation | A traceable digital identity and lineage | Physical segregation or custody |
| Allocation | Quantity reserved against an order under controls | Stock movement, release or dispatch |
| Collection/dispatch event | Reported event with actor, time, location and evidence | Carrier acceptance or legal export permission unless sourced from competent party |
| Shipment milestone | Provider-reported status and reference | Customs release, delivery or payment unless verified by the relevant source |
| Payment status | Instruction/reference/status from authorised provider or buyer/seller evidence | Platform receipt, holding, transfer or settlement of funds |

## Facility capability levels
1. Digital coordination site: connectivity, onboarding, records and training.
2. Producer service point: only specified intake coordination, weighing, identification, photography and document capture after permission, equipment and SOP checks.
3. Custody/storage/processing site: separate written authority, insurance, site controls, staff competence, safety and regulatory validation.
4. Inspection/laboratory/certification site: competent/accredited provider and precise scope; never inferred from premises.

A candidate ICTA hub, TTI, TVC or other site is not appointed by appearing in a plan. A facility may be suitable for one function and not another. Service sessions, equipment and operators must be scoped and time-bounded. Changes require a recorded assessment and approval.
