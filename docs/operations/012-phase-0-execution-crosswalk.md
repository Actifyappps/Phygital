## 1. Disposition

The existing `phase-0-work-map-and-dependencies.md` captures the high-level workstreams and stop conditions. This companion records the detailed 10-work-package sequence, indicative 20-working-day plan, evidence-quality rules and separation between Phase 0 conclusion and live-pilot authorisation. It is a crosswalk, not a parallel execution register.

The Notion Evidence Register and its operating protocol remain the single operational source for evidence status, ownership and closure. Do not maintain a second status ledger in GitHub.

## 2. Indicative work-package sequence

The source proposes four weeks / 20 working days from an authorised kickoff. External response times may extend this window. Missing evidence must not be converted into an assumption to preserve the schedule.

| WP-2 Buyer discovery and specification validation | 3–10 | Wholesale buyer interviews, written product specifications, terms, quality and evidence requirements | Interview evidence, buyer qualification and at least one sufficiently detailed written specification; WP-0/WP-1 |

Work packages may overlap only where dependencies permit. WP-2 and WP-3 can run in parallel after outreach authority and shortlist preparation. The sequence is not a reason to skip prerequisites.

## 3. Indicative sequence is not a status report

The 20-day window is not a kickoff date, commitment or delivery guarantee. The following are explicitly unresolved until authorised evidence exists:
- kickoff date, sponsor and named workstream owners;
- county/geography, commodity, destination and hub;
- buyer/producer contacts and outreach permission;
- legal seller/exporter and contracting arrangement;
- provider availability, licences and commercial terms;
- budget, staff time and funding;
- regulatory determinations and partner agreements;
- defensible numeric thresholds.

Kericho, one commodity, one hub and a limited buyer/producer cohort are planning hypotheses, not confirmed selections.

## 4. Evidence Register operating discipline

For each action:
1. Link it to the relevant CV/E evidence row in the live Notion register.
2. Confirm the responsible role; assign a named person only through an authorised decision.
3. Record the claim being tested, source, authority, date, method, limitations and confidentiality/consent conditions.
4. Retain the original evidence or stable controlled reference.
5. Record reviewer comments and the actual closure test.
6. Update status only through the Evidence Register's authorised protocol.

Evidence classes must remain distinguishable:
- **Declared:** supplied by a party, not independently checked.
- **Hub-recorded:** entered or observed by an authorised hub role; not automatically independently verified.
- **Device-measured:** linked to an identified instrument, calibration/maintenance status, operator, date and method.
- **Independently verified:** checked by a competent independent party, with identity, method, date, scope and report retained.

A prospect list is not buyer demand; a verbal estimate is not confirmed supply; a facility discussion is not site permission; a provider brochure is not verified licensing; a draft schema is not a tested database; a mock shipment dossier is not export readiness.

## 5. Phase 0 completion versus live-pilot gates

Phase 0 can legitimately conclude with HOLD or NO-GO if evidence is missing or a core assumption fails. A reasoned negative finding is a valid Phase 0 output.

A Phase 0 GO only supports the next preparation stage. It does not authorise live trading. A separate written pilot authorisation must follow evidence of, at minimum:
- selected commodity and destination;
- qualified buyer and written specification;
- validated producer/cooperative supply, authority, consent, traceability and product rights;
- settled legal role, contracting chain, liability and required permissions;
- contractually clear hub access, custody, staffing, security, costs and responsibilities;
- confirmed quality, inspection, packaging, logistics, customs and destination requirements;
- appropriately licensed payment/provider arrangements and approved funds-flow;
- credible unit economics and payer willingness;
- technical acceptance in a secured environment;
- approved privacy, security, consent, retention and incident controls;
- approved SOPs, rejection/dispute/claims, insurance and stop-loss controls;
- written pilot scope, parties, dates, limits, risk owners and exit conditions.

## 6. ChatGPT preparation versus external authority

Repository analysis, draft crosswalks, test design, synthetic scenarios, issue identification and preparation of outreach instruments can be prepared as analytical work. They do not replace:
- sponsor decisions and appointments;
- permission to contact or represent the programme;
- buyer/producer commitments and factual validation;
- facility access, institutional approvals or executed agreements;
- legal, tax, regulatory or professional opinions;
- provider licensing and contracts;
- actual database/browser execution by an operator;
- independent review and formal acceptance.
