# Counterparty validation checklist

**Sources:** Deliverables 007 and 013. Use only for structured discovery; an interview is not an appointment, purchase commitment, licence or partner agreement.

## Buyer / importer
- Legal entity, country, identity source and interviewee role.
- Purchasing authority and decision process.
- Product form, grade/specification, quantity, seasonality, delivery window and destination.
- Price basis, currency/date, delivery terms and Incoterm review by qualified trade professional.
- Supplier documentation, origin, batch traceability, sampling, testing, inspection and rejection conditions.
- Packaging, transport mode, route, handling, insurance and required shipment milestones.
- Payment terms, payer/payee, provider/bank and documentation.
- Confidentiality, data-sharing permission and follow-up owner.
- Classify interest B0–B6 using evidence; a letter of interest is not automatically a purchase order or binding commitment.

## Producer / cooperative / aggregator
- Organisation identity, governance, membership, representative mandate and disclosure authority.
- Source location, product form/variety, production calendar and seasonal profile.
- Actual production/available stock distinguished from forecast and historic volume.
- Quantity, unit, method, evidence class and measurement provenance.
- Existing commitments, aggregation, collection and competing channels.
- Quality, traceability, handling, storage, processing and packaging capacity.
- Price expectations, costs, fees, net proceeds and willingness to participate.
- Consent/purpose and minimum necessary member-level data.
- Readiness classification, unresolved gaps and evidence owner.

## Hub / facility / provider
- Legal operator, ownership, written permission and exact service scope.
- Digital infrastructure, power/connectivity, premises, accessibility and safety.
- Staff competence, named lead, supervision and operating hours.
- Equipment inventory, calibration, maintenance and security.
- Commodity-specific suitability, storage/handling capability and regulatory approvals where applicable.
- Insurance, liability allocation, operating cost, service pricing and funding.
- Privacy, cyber controls, records retention, incident/complaint procedures.
- Provider licence/registration, service capability, SLA, data processing, security, business continuity and exit terms.
- Do not record a candidate as approved/contracted without evidence and an authorised decision.

## Evidence discipline
Record interview date, source, interviewer, participant role, authority check, evidence references, evidence class E1–E4, acquisition type, confidence, limitations, follow-up and reviewer. Never turn a verbal indication into a confirmed buyer, supplier, hub, exporter or provider.
