# Demand mapping and market intelligence

**Source:** Concept Paper v1.1, Technology Pillar 4; 024 §6.14; 024-R F-12.  
**Status:** descriptive reporting capability in the baseline; forecasting and SMS advisories are deferred.

## Initial/pilot scope
- Descriptive counts and trends for enquiries, qualified buyer requirements, quotations, accepted orders and completed/closed transactions.
- Price observations with currency, unit, commodity/product form, quality/grade, destination/route, time period, source and limitations.
- Seasonality summaries only where there are sufficient comparable observations and provenance.
- Buyer-demand versus producer-supply compatibility gaps, clearly distinguishing expressed interest from committed demand and declarations from verified supply.
- Producer and buyer outcomes: producer time/out-of-pocket cost, data-entry burden, net proceeds/payment timing where lawfully evidenced, buyer evidence usability, rejection/loss/deduction rates, support burden and exception resolution.
- Cost and revenue per completed transaction only after approved commercial definitions and source records exist.

## Data and governance rules
- Each metric has a definition, owner, calculation version, source context, unit, cohort, time window, exclusions, known bias and refresh time.
- Distinguish activity metrics (accounts, listings, enquiries) from outcomes (accepted orders, fulfilled transactions, producer proceeds and buyer repeat intent).
- Do not infer demand from page views, listing views or unqualified enquiries.
- Do not publish buyer-specific demand, commercially sensitive prices or identifiable producer/member information without an approved purpose and access scope.
- Do not set numerical performance targets before baseline evidence and stakeholder review.
- Corrections to source records must preserve metric lineage and explain historical restatement.

## Deferred forecasting and SMS advisories
Forecasts require enough reliable, representative and lawfully obtained historical data; back-testing against observed outcomes; uncertainty intervals; bias review; human interpretation; versioning and an explicit owner. SMS advisories require recipient eligibility, purpose/lawful basis, consent/preferences where applicable, privacy-safe content, provider due diligence, opt-out/suppression and delivery-failure handling. Neither capability is part of the initial pilot merely because the concept paper identifies it as a later option.
