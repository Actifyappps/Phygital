# Phase-gated capability architecture

**Sources:** Concept Paper v1.1; Technical Development Manual v3.0; 001/005; 024-R; TSBBF.  
**Status:** Capability boundary map, not implementation authorization.

## Baseline digital coordination capability
Identity and organisation; scoped authority; product and supply declarations; batch/evidence records; buyer enquiry; quotation and accepted terms; order lineage; reservation semantics; documents; payment-status records; shipment milestone records; audit, support and reporting—only within the approved phase and synthetic/non-production scope.

## Later capabilities — design seams only
- Connected measurement devices and IoT ingestion.
- Blockchain anchoring or digital product passport extensions.
- Provider-led escrow, FX or payment initiation.
- Automated customs, government, laboratory, carrier or banking integration.
- AI demand forecasting and advanced analytics.
- Multi-country, multi-commodity and multi-hub scale.

Each later capability requires a separate evidence case, business owner, legal/regulatory review, security/privacy assessment, provider/technology evaluation, TSBBF disposition, cost model, ADR and acceptance gate. No stub endpoint, folder, vendor SDK or UI control should imply activation.

## Two-axis gate
Every roadmap record must show both physical/pilot maturity and software delivery maturity. Neither axis automatically unlocks the other.
