# Phase-gated capability architecture

**Sources:** Concept Paper v1.1; Technical Development Manual v3.0; 001/005; 018-A; 024-R; TSBBF.  
**Status:** Capability boundary map, not implementation authorization.

## Four governing technology pillars

| Concept Paper pillar | Initial/pilot digital deliverable | Later capability | Required gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Decentralized quality assurance and aggregation | Hub service/intake records, batch identity, hub-recorded measurements, reviewed evidence, inventory controls; accredited lab results only where buyer/route requires and competent provider is qualified | Connected scales, moisture meters and other devices | Hub workflow validated; device accuracy/calibration and provider competence confirmed; source and provenance retained |
| 2. Digital Product Passport | QR-linked batch page with approved origin, evidence and verification-status projection backed by auditable source records | Blockchain anchoring | Buyer/partner requirement or measurable trust benefit justifies cost; privacy, security, revocation and source-of-truth controls approved |
| 3. Payment and escrow rails | Payment terms, transaction references and manual/provider-verified reconciliation through an appropriately licensed partner | Provider-led escrow, FX conversion and farmer/cooperative payouts | Legal/commercial validation; licensed provider contracted; funds-flow and safeguarding responsibilities confirmed; acceptance tests passed |
| 4. Demand mapping and market intelligence | Descriptive reporting on enquiries, orders, prices and seasonality using reliable, lawfully obtained data | Forecasting and SMS advisories to county networks | Sufficient reliable data; lawful purpose/consent and recipient rules; forecast accuracy tested against outcomes; separate sponsor and technical gate |

## Baseline digital coordination capability

Identity and organisation; scoped authority; product and supply declarations; batch/evidence records; buyer enquiry; quotation and accepted terms; order lineage; reservation semantics; documents; payment-status records; shipment milestone records; audit, support and reporting—only within the approved phase and synthetic/non-production scope.

The four pillars are preserved as strategic architecture. Their pilot deliverables are not all live or authorised merely because a repository home exists.

## Later capabilities — design seams only

- Connected measurement devices and IoT ingestion.
- Blockchain anchoring or advanced passport extensions.
- Provider-led escrow, FX or payment initiation/payout.
- Automated customs, government, laboratory, carrier or banking integration.
- AI demand forecasting and automated SMS advisories.
- Multi-country, multi-commodity and multi-hub scale.

Each later capability requires a separate evidence case, business owner, legal/regulatory review, security/privacy assessment, provider/technology evaluation, TSBBF disposition, cost model, ADR and acceptance gate. No stub endpoint, folder, vendor SDK or UI control should imply activation.

## Two-axis gate

Every roadmap record must show both physical/pilot maturity and software delivery maturity. Neither axis automatically unlocks the other.
