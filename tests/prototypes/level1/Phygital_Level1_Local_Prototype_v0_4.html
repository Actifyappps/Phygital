Purpose and boundary

This local rehearsal checks whether synthetic participant, authority, batch, evidence, enquiry, quotation, order, allocation, exception and mock-dossier records can be linked and reconciled.

In scope: test-only records, batch arithmetic, evidence labels, private QR-style summary, draft quotation, simulated seller review, simulated order/allocation, mock shipment dossier, exception handling and audit events.

Out of scope: live onboarding, physical stock, public QR pages, real listings, contracts, payments, escrow, FX, customs, shipment, production deployment, authentication, database persistence and external integrations.
